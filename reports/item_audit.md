## 4. Facts and definitions still to verify against a primary source

- [ ] `abortion_rule` definition of *viability* (American College of Obstetricians and Gynecologists, periviability guidance)
- [ ] `min_wage`: Federal minimum wage is $7.25/hour, unchanged since July 24, 2009 (US Department of Labor, Wage and Hour Division)
- [ ] `handgun_purchase`: Federal background checks are required for sales by federally licensed dealers, not most private sales (Gun Control Act of 1968; Brady Handgun Violence Prevention Act (1993); ATF)
- [ ] `status_policy`: Pew estimates 14 million unauthorized immigrants as of July 2023 (a record), with a likely decline of up to 1 million in the first half of 2025; DHS estimated about 11 million for January 2022 (Pew Research Center, 'U.S. Unauthorized Immigrant Population Reached a Record 14 Million in 2023' (Aug 21, 2025); DHS Office of Homeland Security Statistics)
- [ ] `low_income_tax`: Roughly 40% of US households owe no federal income tax in a typical year (Tax Policy Center, 'Who doesn't pay federal income tax?' (annual estimates))
- [ ] `corporate_minimum_tax`: Corporate alternative minimum tax of 15% of adjusted financial statement income for corporations averaging over $1 billion, effective tax years after 2022 (Inflation Reduction Act of 2022, Public Law 117-169, sec. 10101)
- [ ] `wealth_tax`: Households with net worth above $50 million are roughly 0.05%-0.1% of US households (Federal Reserve Survey of Consumer Finances; Saez & Zucman wealth tax estimates)
- [ ] `estate_tax_level`: Fewer than 0.1% of estates owe federal estate tax (Joint Committee on Taxation; Tax Policy Center estimates)
- [ ] `child_benefit_level`: Child Tax Credit $2,200 per child from tax year 2025, indexed for inflation; refundable portion limited and requires earned income (Public Law 119-21 (2025); IRS)
- [ ] `right_to_work`: 26 states have right-to-work laws (Michigan repealed its law effective Feb 2024) (National Conference of State Legislatures; Michigan Public Act 8 of 2023)
- [ ] `licensing`: About 20-22% of employed people held an active professional certification or license, mostly government-required licenses (US Bureau of Labor Statistics, Current Population Survey (certification and licensing))
- [ ] `medicaid_work`: Public Law 119-21 requires expansion adults 19-64 to show 80 hours/month of work, community service, or school, with exemptions; states must implement by Jan 1, 2027 (Public Law 119-21 (2025), sec. 71119; KFF)
- [ ] `ss_shortfall`: 2025 Trustees Report: OASI trust fund depletion 2033 (77% payable); combined OASDI 2034 (81% payable) (2025 Social Security Trustees Report)
- [ ] `rent_vouchers`: Only about 1 in 4 households eligible for federal rental assistance receives it (Center on Budget and Policy Priorities analysis of HUD data)
- [ ] `border_device_search`: CBP basic device searches need no suspicion; advanced (forensic) searches need reasonable suspicion or national security concern (CBP Directive 3340-049A (2018))
- [ ] `minors_transition`: United States v. Skrmetti (June 2025) upheld Tennessee's ban; about 27 states restrict gender-transition care for minors (605 U.S. ___ (2025); KFF / Movement Advancement Project tracking)
- [ ] `ten_commandments`: Louisiana (2024), Arkansas and Texas (2025) passed classroom display laws; federal courts have blocked some (State statutes; Roake v. Brumley (5th Cir.))
- [ ] `legal_immigration_level`: Roughly 1.0-1.2 million lawful permanent residents admitted per year in recent fiscal years (DHS Office of Homeland Security Statistics, Yearbook of Immigration Statistics)
- [ ] `skills_vs_family`: Family-sponsored and immediate-relative categories account for roughly 60-65% of new green cards (DHS Yearbook of Immigration Statistics)
- [ ] `aid_in_dying`: Medical aid in dying authorized in about 12-13 jurisdictions including DC; New York and Illinois enacted laws in 2025-26 (Death with Dignity National Center; news reports (Dec 2025))
- [ ] `ballot_measures`: 26 states provide for statewide citizen-initiated ballot measures or popular referendums; no national initiative process (Ballotpedia; National Conference of State Legislatures)
- [ ] `defense_spending`: US national defense spending roughly $850-950 billion per year (FY2025-26); exceeds the next ~9 countries combined (CBO; SIPRI Military Expenditure Database; Peter G. Peterson Foundation)
- [ ] `foreign_aid_level`: US foreign aid historically about 1% of federal outlays (KFF; Congressional Research Service)
- [ ] `international_courts`: The US signed but did not ratify the Rome Statute and withdrew its signature in 2002; sanctions on ICC officials imposed in 2020 and 2025 (UN Treaty Collection; Executive Order 14203 (2025))
